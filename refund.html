Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. General Conditions

At BerdSend, we strive to ensure all mobile top-ups and money transfers are processed accurately and delivered instantly. Because of the digital nature of our services, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

2. Eligible Cases for Refund

  • Transactions that failed due to a system error on our end but where your account was charged.
  • Mobile top-up sent to an incorrect number caused by a technical glitch in our platform (not user input error).
  • Duplicate transactions processed accidentally.

3. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Transactions successfully delivered to the correct recipient phone number or mobile wallet provided by the user.
  • Delays caused by third-party mobile carriers (e.g., Digicel, Natcom, Claro, Altice) or network outages.

4. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you qualify for a refund, please contact our support team via our contact page with your transaction ID, registered email, and proof of payment within 48 hours of the transaction.
